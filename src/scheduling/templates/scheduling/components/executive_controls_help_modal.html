How Controls works

What is it?

Controls is Castor’s schedule / model / quantity readiness workspace. It helps you see schedule performance readiness, baseline and progress availability, link coverage, model readiness, and quantity readiness — not a company cost dashboard.

Workspace layout

Use the command bar and stats strip for a quick readiness read, the left navigator to jump categories, the center readiness grid for status and next actions, and the right inspector for the selected control.

Schedule Performance Indicator

When available, Schedule Performance Indicator (SPI) and planned / earned schedule progress come from schedule and progress inputs. Open Schedule Performance for the detail view. This is not financial Cost EVM.

Link Coverage / 4D readiness

Link Coverage counts Applied / Confirmed activity–model links. Task coverage and entity coverage use different denominators and are not contradictory.

Model & Quantities

Model readiness reflects the indexed IFC inventory. Quantity readiness points to IFC model quantities — not commercial BOQ.

Company cost source

Company-cost metrics (CPI, Actual Cost, EAC, ETC, VAC, TCPI) stay unavailable. Castor has no ERP, invoice, QS, payroll, procurement, or company cost ledger integration. ResourceAssignment.actual_cost is schedule assignment data only — not company spend.

Caveats

Baseline fields from import are not a contractual baseline claim. Delay / critical indicators are analytical, not contractual claims. Detail sections below the workspace remain available for deeper review.
