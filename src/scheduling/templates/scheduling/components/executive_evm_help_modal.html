How EVM Analytics works

What is it?

Current-point schedule and cost performance from the live schedule snapshot — SPI, CPI, PV, EV, AC, and derived as-of S-curves.

Derived vs historical

Curves are Derived As-of S-Curves — mathematically reconstructed from current task rows. They are not imported historical performance and may change after re-import.

Cost EVM vs Schedule Performance

Cost EVM requires authoritative actual cost. When unavailable, Schedule Performance uses duration-weighted progress — never labelled as monetary EVM.

Caveats

  • Missing metrics show as Unavailable — not zero.
  • CPI near 1.0 should be read with tolerance context.
  • Historical SPI/CPI trends require AnalyticalSnapshot (not yet available).
