How Schedule Performance & Readiness works

What is it?

Schedule Performance & Readiness shows schedule/progress indicators (Schedule Performance Indicator, planned vs earned schedule progress) from the live schedule snapshot. It is not a financial Cost EVM or company cost-performance dashboard.

Company cost source

CPI, Actual Cost, EAC, ETC, VAC, and TCPI stay Unavailable — requires a company cost source. Castor has no ERP, invoice, QS, payroll, procurement, or company cost ledger integration. ResourceAssignment.actual_cost is schedule assignment data only — not company spend.

Derived vs historical

Curves are Derived As-of S-Curves — reconstructed from current task rows. They are not imported historical performance and may change after re-import.

Caveats

  • Missing company-cost metrics show as Unavailable — not zero.
  • Schedule Performance Indicator is based on schedule/progress inputs only.
  • Historical SPI trends require AnalyticalSnapshot (not yet available).
