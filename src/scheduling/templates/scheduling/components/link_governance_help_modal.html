How Applied Links Works

What is it?

Applied Links is where you confirm or ignore schedule–model link suggestions, review link coverage, and see applied / confirmed bindings used by Time View and Controls.

How to use it

  • Use Suggest Links on Links to generate candidates.
  • Open Applied Links to Confirm Link or Ignore Suggestion.
  • Applied / Confirmed links drive Time View highlights and link coverage.

Who can do what

Project editors can confirm individual links. Bulk confirmation may require owner access. Viewers can inspect coverage without confirming.

Caveats

  • Confidence scores do not auto-confirm links.
  • Ignore Suggestion records history and keeps the suggestion out of applied reads.
  • This is link management for 4D — not ERP, BOQ, or commercial 5D.
